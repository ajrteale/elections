Beechwood and Heath

Halton council, Cheshire; caused by the death of Liberal Democrat councillor Margaret Ratcliffe. We now cross south of the Border to reach Runcorn, a Cheshire New Town which lies on the south bank of the Mersey estuary. The Mersey and the Ship Canal squeeze through a rather narrow gap here, which is overlooked by the summit of Runcorn Hill: this is one of a number of sandstone summits here, although it is not on the same scale as Frodsham Hill or Helsby Hill to the south-west. Running south-west from here we find the housing of Runcorn Heath next to the railway line from Liverpool, and then the Beechwood Estate next to the M56 motorway junction. The land here falls away rapidly towards the Weaver Navigation, which is crossed by high viaducts on the motorway and on the adjacent Chester to Warrington railway line. Much of Runcorn is New Town development built as Liverpool overspill, but Beechwood and Heath ward is different. Beechwood's housing in particular was developed privately. In the 2021 census Beechwood and Heath was in the top 100 wards in England and Wales for owner-occupation (88.5%), and in general it is a far more middle-class area than Runcorn as a whole. This area also votes in a far more middle-class way than Runcorn as a whole. In local elections this is Runcorn's Lib Dem hotspot and has been for many years. Beechwood and Heath has consistently voted Lib Dem since its creation in 2021; of the predcessor wards, the Lib Dems won Heath every year from 2002 to 2019 with the exception of a Labour win in 2015, while the smaller Beechwood ward voted Lib Dem until the formation of the Coalition and Labour thereafter. The political context in Runcorn changed with the extremely narrow Reform UK gain in the Runcorn and Helsby by-election last year. The by-election winner Sarah Pochin, who was the first and still the only woman to be elected as an MP on the Reform UK ticket, subsequently had her win upheld by the Election Court following an ill-advised legal challenge by the candidate of the English Constitution Party. She took the seat over from Labour's Mike Amesbury, who had resigned following an assult conviction. Reform UK went on to win all but one of the wards in Runcorn at the May 2026 Halton council election, with Beechwood and Heath being the one that got away: the Lib Dems held off Reform UK here by 45-40. It may be a significant factor in that result that Labour - who had finished a strong second here between 2021 and 2024 - did not field a candidate in Beechwood and Heath in May. The Halton Lib Dems have big shoes to fill here after their group leader died in August. Margaret Ratcliffe was a long-serving councillor who had been on Halton council for all but four years since it became a unitary council in 1997: she represented Heath ward from 1997 until 2015 when she lost her seat to Labour, but returned in 2019 and then transferred to Beechwood and Heath following boundary changes in 2021. Ratcliffe was mayor of Halton in 2013-14. Defending this seat for the Lib Dems is Diane Inch, who has regularly stood in Norton North ward in recent years. Reform UK have selected Neil Thornton, who has written a number of books on military history including a biography of local First World War hero Thomas "Todger" Jones VC, whose statue stands in this ward opposite the town's war memorial. Also on the ballot are independent candidate Dan Clarke who has stood in this ward on the Conservative, Liberal Party and Libertarian tickets in the last three local elections, Owen Ashworth for Labour, Ash Corcoran for Restore Britain and Luke Hanson for the Conservatives.
