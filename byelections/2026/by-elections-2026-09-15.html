"All the right votes, but not necessarily in the right order"
One by-election on Tuesday 15th September 2026:

East Markham

Bassetlaw council, Nottinghamshire; caused by the resignation of Conservative councillor Gary Dinsdale.

Andrew's Previews is going to start this week with a rare Tuesday poll. The reason for this appears to be that there is a full meeting of Bassetlaw council scheduled for Thursday evening, and the unusual Tuesday scheduling of this by-election means that the winner can hit the ground running straight away.

The Bassetlaw district at the northern end of Nottinghamshire is dominated by the old coalmining town of Worksop and the smaller town of Retford on the old Great North Road. East Markham ward covers nine rural and agricultural parishes to the south of Retford, but the only settlement here of any significant size is East Markham itself which is bypassed by the A57 road between the Markham Moor junction and Lincoln. A majority of the electors here live in East Markham parish.

Markham Moor marks the point where the modern A1 (which passes well to the west of Retford) diverges from the old Great North Road through Retford. It is a landmark for A1 travellers thanks to the presence of the Scorer Building, a hyperbolic paraboloid roof which was built in 1959-60 as an exercise in the possibilities of sexy concrete architecture. Originally the roof sheltered a petrol station, but a Happy Eater restaurant opened under the canopy in 1982; after a few changes of hands and some years in the 2010s standing empty, it's now a branch of Starbucks.

This area last went to the polls in the 2025 Nottinghamshire county council elections as part of the Tuxford county division, which was a fairly narrow Reform UK gain from the Conservatives. The local MP might well have been unhappy about this at time, as this was before he defected to Reform UK: East Markham is one of five Bassetlaw wards located in the Newark parliamentary seat, which has been represented since a 2014 by-election by Robert Jenrick.

The Bassetlaw wards in Jenrick's constituency form the core of the Conservative opposition on the Labour-controlled Bassetlaw council. The Tories gained East Markham ward from the Liberal Democrats in 2003 and haven't been seriously here since - in fact, in the 2019 election the Conservative councillor the ward's long-serving Conservative councillor John Ogle wasn't challenged at all, and he was re-elected for his fifth term of office unopposed. Ogle retired at the 2023 election, and his successor Gary Dinsdale enjoyed a 66-21 lead over Labour.

Dinsdale submitted his resignation in July, with the local press noting that his council attendance had been poor (although not poor enough to trigger the six-month non-attendance rule). The defending Conservative candidate James Purle will be well aware that this by-election is likely to be a tougher fight than the last two decades' results for East Markham suggest, as in May 2025 he lost two elections in the district to Reform UK - standing for the county council in Worksop West and in a Bassetlaw council by-election in Sturton ward. Also returning from last year's Sturton by-election is Labour's Colette Roberts, a former further education teacher who ran the office of a Nottinghamshire MP for over 20 years. Completing the ballot paper are the Green Party's David Bean and Reform UK's Matthew Evans, who is a parish councillor elsewhere within Bassetlaw district and stood in 2023 as the Conservative candidate for the neighbouring Welbeck ward.
